Privacy policy

Bookmark Sync has no server and collects nothing. The developer never receives your data.

What it reads: your browser bookmarks (titles, URLs and folders), and a GitHub token, owner and repo name that you enter yourself.

Where it goes: bookmarks are sent only to api.github.com, into the repository you configured, using your token. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

What is stored locally: the token, repo settings and a snapshot of the last synced state, in the browser's extension storage (or ~/.bookmark-sync on a Mac). The token is not encrypted there; scope it to the one bookmarks repo.

No analytics, ads or tracking. Bookmark data is never sold, shared or used for anything but syncing.
